Features

More than a player.
Built around you.

Everything listed here is described in the project's own README and has a module in the source code.

Background playback

Keep listening while the screen is off or another app is open.

Live synced lyrics

Follow lyrics in time with the music, with translation and romanization options.

Local and YouTube Music

Play files stored on your device, or sign in to bring in your YouTube Music library.

Audio controls

Loudness normalization, tempo, pitch and speed, crossfade, and system equalizer integration.

Spotify playlist import

Bring a playlist over from Spotify instead of rebuilding it by hand.

Scrobbling and presence

Last.fm scrobbling, ListenBrainz history sync and Discord rich presence.

Listening statistics

See your top songs, artists and listening time whenever you want.

Material 3 and dynamic color

Album-art powered colors, up to 9 player styles and deep customization.

About VYRA

Music should
feel like yours.

VYRA is built around a simple idea: the player should disappear and the music should stay. Fewer detours, more atmosphere, and settings that let you decide how it looks and sounds.

It is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, so you can read the code, follow development and contribute.
